# Image prompt set

Mode: OpenAI built-in image generation.  
Use case: `photorealistic-natural` for real-property frames, `stylized-concept` / `sketch-to-render` for blueprint frames, and `compositing` for transition frames.  
Native frame: portrait 3:4, 1086×1448.

## Shared identity lock

One believable, ordinary late-1990s or early-2000s two-storey suburban family house, about 185 m²: simple rectangular footprint, attached single-car garage on the left, modest recessed covered porch at center-right, dark-green front door, beige painted stucco, a narrow red-brown brick base, charcoal asphalt-shingle roof, white vinyl windows, concrete drive, patchy maintained lawn, mature trees and neighboring homes partly visible. Authentic MLS photography, normal material wear and practical construction details; never a mansion, luxury renovation, conceptual building or glossy CGI render. No people, cars, signs, house numbers, text, logos or watermarks.

## Assets

### 01 — Empty blueprint

Deep navy blueprint paper `#16233F` fills the portrait frame. Only a faint precise millimeter grid in translucent draft white; subtle paper fiber, flat illumination, empty center. No drawing, border, title block, text or tools.

### 02 — Blueprint elevation

Using the real exterior as immutable geometry, recreate the exact facade as a face-on CAD elevation: draft-white `#EAF2FF` one-pixel lines, restrained hatching, dimension ticks, faint grid, and one cold green-biased chartreuse `#D1EF17` centerline. Preserve roof, garage, porch, columns, door and every front window. No readable labels or photoreal materials.

### 03 — Blueprint axonometric

Using the same real exterior and elevation, tilt the exact home into a 25-degree three-quarter axonometric wireframe on the same blueprint paper. Preserve all identity anchors. Add one chartreuse route from driveway to front door and through the structure. Crisp CAD, no glow, no text.

### 04 — Line to matter

Using the real exterior and matching axonometric, show one in-world transformation state in the same camera angle. Driveway, lawn and lower floor are fully real; upper floor and roof remain crisp CAD. Material grows directly from connected plotted lines, not from a split-screen edge. A chartreuse route is the ignition path and the blueprint paper develops into cloudy daylight from the house silhouette outward.

### 05 — Real exterior

Authentic eye-level listing photo of the shared house from across the driveway, 28 mm lens, stable verticals, soft cloudy late-afternoon light, ordinary neighboring context, real wear and slightly imperfect lawn. Complete facade and roof visible. No cinematic fog or luxury styling.

### 06 — Blueprint floor plan

Plausible matching ground-floor plan viewed perfectly top-down. Garage front-left, porch and foyer front center-right, modest living room behind the large front window, compact kitchen and dining at rear, central stair and powder room. Draft-white CAD on deep navy grid with one chartreuse route from porch to living room. No readable labels or numbers.

### 07 — Living room

Authentic modest front living room from the foyer: gray three-seat sofa, muted olive chair, practical oak coffee table, beige low-pile rug, TV console and floor lamp. Warm-oak laminate, beige walls, white trim, standard eight-foot ceiling and the correct large front window. Soft cloudy daylight plus one warm practical; real MLS quality, not designer staging.

### 08 — Kitchen and dining

Same home and finish tier at the rear: compact U-shaped kitchen, clean slightly dated off-white shaker cabinets, light laminate counters, white tile backsplash, ordinary stainless appliances, small movable wood island and a simple six-seat oak dining table. Warm-oak laminate, beige walls, standard ceiling, natural mixed light, attainable and lightly used.

### 09 — Backyard

Real listing photograph looking toward the matching rear facade: beige stucco, brick base, charcoal roof, white windows, one ordinary patio door, small poured-concrete patio with basic four-seat table, wooden privacy fence, patchy maintained grass, mature trees and simple shrubs. No pool, deck, outdoor kitchen or luxury landscaping.

### 10 — Proof overlay

Keep the real front exterior unchanged. Plot razor-crisp draft-white lines directly on matching roof edges, garage, porch columns, front door and window frames. Add a few chartreuse dimension arrows aligned with real levels. CAD lines are attached to their material twins, with zero glow and no geometry mismatch or readable text.
