# Homie 30s Launch Film — Keyframe Prompt Set

All frames use the same visual system: 3:4 portrait, warm white `#F5F2EC`, Homie sage `#748668`, charcoal `#161614`, crisp 2D/2.5D UI objects, soft premium studio lighting, rounded cards, restrained shadows, and the official Homie wordmark. All important content stays inside a centered 4:5 safety region. Avoid people, subtitles, fake text, watermarks, and additional accent colors.

1. **Listing still** — One modern-property photo card centered in white space, separating into three depth layers as a sage motion line enters from the lower left.
2. **Depth layers** — The same property card fanned into five crisp layers with white speed trails and a tight sage orbit.
3. **Active video** — The layers snap into one vertical player with minimal playback controls and the sage line exiting toward the next scene.
4. **Template library** — Seven real-estate template cards sweep horizontally in perspective; the same property remains visible in the carousel.
5. **Template selected** — The chosen template snaps forward with a sage outline, check indicator, and the exact label “Use template.”
6. **Photo drop** — The selected template compresses into a chip while six photos from the same property fall into a dashed upload zone labeled “Drop property photos” and “6 files.”
7. **Room sequence** — The six photos organize into Exterior, Entry, Living, Kitchen, Bedroom, and Backyard along a fast sage zigzag path ending in a timeline.
8. **Review and approve** — The sequence collapses into a vertical tour player with Review, New version, and a prominent sage approval action.
9. **Brand finale** — The approved player floats above the exact Homie wordmark and the tagline “Make every property move.” while the sage line completes a bold orbit.
