# Homie — Refined Video Template Prompts

All prompts are designed for photorealistic real-estate video. The supplied property photos are immutable visual ground truth. Aspect ratio, duration, and other generation settings are configured manually in-app when a user creates a video — they are intentionally left out of these prompts.

Every template is produced as **two chained clips**, not one:

- **Video 1 — Hook** ends the instant the camera crosses the threshold into the entryway (or, for #11, the instant it phases through the window into the first room). It never describes the rest of the walkthrough.
- **Video 2 — Walkthrough** starts from that exact frame and carries the property tour the rest of the way. Its prompt opens by re-stating the lighting, camera position, and mood the hook ended on, so the two clips read as one uninterrupted shot when stitched.

See **Continuity technique** at the bottom for how to lock the two generations together in production.

For voiceover/narration scripts to pair with these visuals (agent intro, neighborhood tour, market update, testimonials, etc.), see [`HOMIE_NARRATION_SCRIPT_TEMPLATES.md`](HOMIE_NARRATION_SCRIPT_TEMPLATES.md) — a separate template set for what gets said, not what the camera does.

## Continuity technique

To make Video 1 and Video 2 read as one uninterrupted shot when stitched:

- **Generate in order.** Render the Hook first, export its exact final frame as a still image.
- **Chain the frame.** Feed that still into Video 2's generation as the starting/reference frame (image-to-video or first-frame-lock mode) instead of relying on the text prompt alone to match position and lighting.
- **Lock shared parameters.** Keep the same seed family, LUT/color grade, lens/FOV, and camera height setting across both generations — the Video 2 prompts above restate the lighting and camera state in words, but locking generation parameters is what actually prevents drift.
- **Crossfade the audio, not the picture.** Cut the visual hard at the frame boundary; crossfade the last ~0.5s of the hook's sound design into the first ~0.5s of the walkthrough's ambient track so the audio, not the visual, carries the seam.
- **Aspect ratio and duration** are set per-generation in-app, not in the prompt text.

## Recommended shared generation settings

- Visual style: photorealistic, premium real-estate cinematography, physically coherent motion
- Camera: stabilized drone/gimbal movement, controlled motion blur, no abrupt random cuts
- Reference priority: property photos are immutable ground truth
- Avoid: people, text, logos, watermarks, altered architecture, duplicated objects, warped geometry, invented rooms, and unseen property features
