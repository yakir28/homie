# Homie — Promo Video Brief

## What is Homie?

Homie is an AI video creation platform for real estate. It turns existing property listing photos into polished, cinematic home-tour videos. Real-estate agents and office teams can give their listings a professional video presence without filming a new tour, learning video editing, or writing AI prompts.

The core idea is simple: **Your listing photos. A cinematic home tour.**

## Who is it for?

Homie is built for independent real-estate agents and brokerage teams who want to market properties with engaging video. They already have property photos, but turning those photos into compelling content takes time, creative direction, and editing work. Homie makes that process guided and approachable.

## How it works

1. **Bring in a listing.** Import property details and photos from a supported listing source, such as Zillow or Airbnb.
2. **Choose a video style.** Browse curated templates with visual previews and pick the creative direction that suits the property.
3. **Generate the tour.** Homie uses the listing photos and selected template to create a property video. The creative instructions run behind the scenes.
4. **Review and approve.** Watch the result and approve it before downloading and sharing.

The resulting videos help agents present properties on social channels such as Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Stories.

## Why it matters

A listing photo shows a room. A video can help someone imagine moving through it. Homie gives agents a way to bring existing property imagery to life and create more engaging marketing material from assets they already have.

Its main benefits are a simple workflow, curated creative choices, less manual production work, and control over the final result. The agent chooses the style and reviews the video before using it.

## The message this promo should communicate

**Turn listing photos into home tours that move.**

Make the transformation from still property photos to a cinematic video the hero of the promo. The viewer should immediately understand what Homie does, recognize how simple the process is, and want to create a tour for their own listing.

## Suggested promo direction

- **Length:** 30 seconds.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9.
- **Language:** English.
- **Style:** Mixed media, combining property photography, paper cutouts, simple interface cards, and smooth motion.
- **Tone:** Warm, confident, premium, and easy to understand.
- **Visual palette:** Warm ivory, charcoal, and muted sage or olive accents.
- **Music:** Light, modern instrumental music that supports the narration.
- **Brand name:** Homie, pronounced “home-ee.”

Start with flat listing-photo cards, then bring them into a flowing home-tour sequence. Show a simple template selection, a video preview, and an approval moment. End with the Homie name and one clear call to action. Keep on-screen wording brief and readable.


## Accuracy notes for the video creator

Present Homie as a tool that creates videos from existing property photos. Keep the property recognizable throughout the transformation. Sharing happens after the agent reviews and approves the result. Avoid adding unverified prices, generation-time guarantees, sales results, customer counts, direct automatic publishing, or claims of perfect property accuracy. Do not imply an official partnership with listing platforms. Use the Homie name and call to action without inventing a website address.
