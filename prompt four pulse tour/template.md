# Pulse Tour

- Duration: 18 seconds
- Format: 9:16
- Style: high-pace, photorealistic, relatable suburban home
- Generation: four independent 5-second Kling 3.0 image-to-video handles
- Assembly: each handle accelerated to 90% duration, then hard-cut into one 18.1-second master

## Chronological source order

1. High aerial exterior
2. Front approach
3. Living room
4. Kitchen
5. Dining room
6. Primary bedroom
7. Backyard
8. Closing aerial

## Editing rhythm

Fast travel, precise brake, readable feature reveal. Use hard cuts on matched direction. Never generatively morph between different rooms or cross an unseen doorway.

## Continuity lock

Treat every source image as ground truth. Lock architecture, roofline, walls, windows, doors, flooring, cabinetry, appliances, furniture identity, furniture count, decor positions, landscaping, fences, materials, colors, and lighting logic. Animate the camera and subtle real foliage only. Keep each exact scene unchanged from its first frame to its last frame. No redesigned rooms, geometry drift, invented openings, moving furniture, duplicate objects, invented hallways, fake doorway travel, people, text, logos, watermarks, floating 3D-render motion, whip pans, or excessive blur.

## Shot handles

### 1. Aerial to front approach

Controlled drone descent toward the exact house. At peak forward motion, use a clean editorial cut to the exact ground-level walkway and continue a short stabilized push. Stop before the front door fills the frame.

### 2. Living room to kitchen

Restrained three-percent push in the living room with a readable hold. Hard cut on an architectural line into the exact kitchen, followed by a restrained eight-degree arc around the fixed island.

### 3. Dining room to bedroom

Restrained three-percent lateral slider in the dining room. Hard cut on motion into the exact bedroom, followed by a slow three-percent push and hold.

### 4. Backyard to closing aerial

Lateral reveal from behind existing foliage followed by a small pullback. Hard cut to the exact aerial frame, controlled drone pull-up, and final stable hold.
