# City Apartment — Image Reference Set

Format: native portrait 3:4. Generation mode: OpenAI built-in image generation (not Higgsfield).

## Visual continuity

- Mid-rise, eight-story urban building in warm beige brick
- Sixth-floor apartment with dark bronze window frames
- Warm off-white walls, light oak floors, walnut accents
- Natural late-afternoon light and neighboring brick-building views
- Realistic, attainable contemporary design rather than ultra-luxury staging

1. `01-building-exterior.png` — elevated three-quarter street view showing entrance and upper floors.
2. `02-building-lobby.png` — glazed entrance, terrazzo floor, mailboxes, bench and elevator.
3. `03-apartment-entry.png` — entry foyer with direct sightline toward the living room.
4. `04-living-room.png` — living room with balcony door and neighborhood view.
5. `05-kitchen.png` — open kitchen looking back toward the living room.
6. `06-primary-bedroom.png` — primary bedroom from its doorway.
7. `07-bathroom.png` — compact renovated bathroom from its doorway.
8. `08-balcony-view.png` — balcony from inside the open door, facing neighboring buildings.

All prompts emphasized photorealistic real-estate photography, stable verticals, believable dimensions, clear camera paths, no people, no readable text, no logos, and no fantasy or penthouse-luxury elements.
